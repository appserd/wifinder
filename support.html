Common questions

It read the name or password wrong.
Tap the name or password to fix it before you join. Fancy fonts and glare can trick the reader; try again closer and straight on.
It couldn't join a network from a router sticker.
Stickers show the router's factory name and password. If the owner changed them, type the new ones.
It says "Joined" but I'm not online.
iOS can report a join even when the password is wrong. Check the password and try again from the Wallet.
The speed test won't start.
The Speed map only runs on Wi-Fi, so it measures your home and not your phone plan. Turn on Wi-Fi and connect to your network.
Can WiFinder show Wi-Fi networks around me?
No. iOS doesn't allow apps to list nearby networks. WiFinder reads the details from signs, stickers and QR codes instead.
Where is my data?
Only on your iPhone. There's no account. See the privacy policy.
