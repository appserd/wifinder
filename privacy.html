WiFinder

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

The short version

WiFinder does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you save stays on your iPhone.

Camera and photos

When you scan a Wi-Fi sign, receipt, router sticker or QR code, the image is read on your iPhone with Apple's built-in text recognition. Photos are never uploaded or sent anywhere, and WiFinder does not keep them.

Saved networks and passwords

Networks you join are saved on your iPhone only. Passwords are stored in your iPhone's secure Keychain. Your Guest card is stored on your iPhone too. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Speed test

The Speed map measures your connection by downloading and uploading random test data to Cloudflare's public speed test service (speed.cloudflare.com). No personal information is sent: like any internet request, Cloudflare sees your connection's IP address while the test runs. Speed results are saved only on your iPhone. Cloudflare's privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Joining networks

WiFinder asks iOS to join a network using Apple's standard system prompt. Wi-Fi details are passed only to iOS on your device.

Deleting your data

Remove a network in the Wallet, or press and hold a room in the Speed map to remove it. Deleting the app removes all of WiFinder's data.

Children

WiFinder collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
